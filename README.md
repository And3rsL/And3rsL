### 🎙️ What I do

- 📺 I work at **[BMAT](https://www.bmat.com/)** on broadcast media analytics: TV & radio advertising monitoring, airing certification and advertising intelligence.
- ⚙️ Day to day: **data engineering**, **backend development** and **media processing pipelines** — from raw audio/video to enriched, queryable data.
- 🧠 Lately exploring video segmentation (program / break / spot detection) and computer-vision-based brand detection in sports content.
- 🏠 In my spare time I tinker with **home automation** and **Home Assistant** integrations.
